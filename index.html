Roadmap — priorities from this data

Ordered by where the numbers show the most leverage.

01Fix Jayanagar's GBP listing signals

Lowest average rank and fewest top-5 keywords of any branch — review categories, photos, and review velocity for the Jayanagar profile specifically.

02Recover Yelahanka's Navodaya/Sainik listings

Both fell meaningfully this period (17→23 and 3→7) while the rest of the branch held steady — check for a duplicate or suspended competitor listing outranking us.

03Protect Malleshwaram's lead

It's the best-performing GBP branch — keep posting cadence and review responses consistent so this doesn't slip the way its SEO did.

04Close the School Tuition GBP gap

16 of 45 School Tuition branch/keyword cells are still unranked on GBP — services-section and category tagging work here is lower-effort than SEO content and can move faster.
